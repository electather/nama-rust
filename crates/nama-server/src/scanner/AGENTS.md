## Scanner

Walking each Library's folders, storing the video files in them and what their names say they hold, the sidecar subtitles beside them, copies of the images beside them or in a Show's or Season's folder (`LocalImages`), watching the folders for changes, and deleting media.

- **One path for Library changes.** Outside tests, creating, updating or deleting a Library goes through `Scanner::create_library`, `update_library` or `delete_library` in `library_changes.rs`, which alone give a new Library its default title sources, refuse a title-source or Recogniser order it may not ask its Plugins in, reading what they say from `PluginSettings`, and do what must follow it (re-watching folders, cleaning a deleted Library's cached subtitles, Custom Image and copies of the images beside its media) in a task that outlives the caller (ADR 0029). Handlers read through `Libraries`.
- **One path for deleting media.** Every delete of media files, the API's and the cleanup Plugin's, goes through `Scanner::delete_media`, or `Scanner::start_delete` for a Plugin, which starts it without waiting and logs its failure. Callers name a target (`ToDelete`) and the Episodes it may take along; `deleting/doomed.rs` alone works out what that takes, and `Doomed` never leaves `deleting/`. Either refuses a read-only folder, then an Episode taken along that the caller didn't name, checks both again under the Library's scan lock, deletes in a task that outlives the caller, ends the Playback Plans playing what goes first, removes the Versions through `Contents`, logs who deleted what and cancels the open Requests still waiting on it through `Requests::apply` (ADR 0038).
- **A scan writes in batches under the scan lock.** A scan holds the Library's scan lock (`library::ScanLocks`) from reading what is stored through Matching, and stores its changes in batches of `BATCH_SIZE`, so other writes don't wait long behind it.
- **Recognisers see what the name parser couldn't place.** `recognised.rs` asks the Library's enabled Recognisers, in the Library's order, about the files the name parser made no Item of (ADR 0050).
- **Name parser version.** A change to how names are parsed or how Matching decides that could turn an Unmatched Item into a Match bumps `PARSER_VERSION` in `../library/versions.rs`, so the next scan parses stored files again and retries their automatic Unmatched decisions (ADR 0023). So does a change to what Matching stores about an automatic Unmatched decision, such as why it was made, so decisions stored before it get it.
- **Unit tests.** The area's unit tests share the `Fixture` in `scanner_tests.rs` in this folder.

### Neighbours

- Uses: `db`, `events`, `library` (`Contents`, `Libraries` with `Order`, `check_known` and `default_title_sources`, `ScanLocks`, `PARSER_VERSION`, `Artwork`, `CustomImages`, `LocalImages`, and `Titles`, `TitleTarget`, `NotFound`, `known_as`, `named_episodes` and `episode_name` to find what a delete names), `matching` (`Matcher`, `count`), `playback` (`Probing`, `RunningPlans` (`for_tests` in tests), `Sidecars` with `Sidecar`, `VersionSidecars` and `is_sidecar`, and `SubtitleCache` and `Tracks` in tests), `requests`, `plugins`, `plugin_settings` (`PluginSettings`, `for_tests` in tests), `server_settings` (`ServerUrl`), and `images`, `ffmpeg` and `log_capture` in tests.
- Used by: `api` (libraries), `automation`, `browse` (`read_only_folder`), and startup in `lib.rs`.
